          How scores and labels work

          Data source

          Model prediction
          Detected by the trained YOLO11s model (RDD2022 India data) running on uploaded video. Can contain misses and false alarms.
          Synthetic demo
          Generated by scripts/seed_demo.py to demonstrate the workflow. These defects, images, confidences and severities are simulated — nothing was detected.
          GPS: device / reconstructed / simulated
          Device: logged by the phone during the ride. Reconstructed: rebuilt afterwards from a map route and video landmarks; expect ±10–20 m. Simulated: generated for synthetic rides.

          Detection confidence (0–1)

          The detector's own score for a box, averaged over the frames in which the defect was tracked. It is not a calibrated probability of being correct.

          Severity index (0–100)

          A relative index from how large the detection box appears, corrected for distance using an assumed camera mount (1.3 m high, tilted 8°, 50° field of view) on a flat road. Potholes and alligator cracking scale with apparent area; linear cracks with apparent length. Lower detector confidence scales the index down by up to 25%.

          It has not been validated against field measurements, so the dashboard does not show physical sizes. Treat it as a ranking signal, not a measurement.

          Levels: Low < 30 ≤ Medium < 55 ≤ High < 75 ≤ Critical

          Priority score (0–100)

          A hand-set heuristic for ordering the worklist — not a learned or validated model. It is the sum of:

          Severity index × 0.550–55
          Type: pothole / alligator cracking+10 / +6
          Growth — only when "Worsening" (below)up to +20
          Confirmations: 2.5 per ride that detected itup to +10
          Nearby: 1 per other active defect within 60 m (same data source)up to +5
          If possibly repaired× 0.2

          Capped at 100. Every defect's panel lists the exact points it received.

          Change over time

          Worsening
          Detected on 2+ rides at least 7 days apart, and the severity index rose by 10+ points. Nothing else is called worsening.
          Too close to judge
          Multiple rides, but less than 7 days apart.
          No significant change / Fell
          Change smaller than 10 points, or a drop of 10+ (check for partial repair).
          Seen on 1 ride
          No history yet.

          Lighting check

          Before detection, 24 frames are sampled across each uploaded video and the brightness of the road ahead is measured (0–255). The model's daytime training images have a median of about 95, and 95% are above 57.

          Too dark
          Half or more of the sampled frames are below 45. Expect missed defects and false alarms; re-record in daylight.
          Low light
          At least 30% of sampled frames are below 60. Results are less reliable than usual.

          The warning does not change any detection or score; it tells you how far to trust them.

          Ride confirmations & repairs

          Confirmed on N rides: N separate rides detected damage of the same family (pothole vs. crack) within 10 m. Synthetic and model data are never merged.

          Possibly repaired: at least 2 later rides passed within 12 m without detecting it.

          Recommended action

          Set by severity level only: Critical → patch within 48 h; High → within 7 days; Medium → this month's resurfacing cycle; Low → monitor. These are suggested defaults, not municipal service levels.
